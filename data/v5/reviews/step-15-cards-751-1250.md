Status: complete candidate/review/register pass for the practical-1250 milestone.

Expand the everyday Iranian curriculum from 750 to 1,250 cards without letting generic written frequency dominate. This stage emphasizes work, school, health, technology, feelings, errands, and broader practical comprehension while continuing to favor productive constructions over isolated dictionary words.

## Batch ordering fix

Step 15 exposed an infrastructure bug: lexicographic filename sorting would place `core-1051...` before `core-301...`. The effective-deck loaders now sort batch files by the numeric start position embedded in each filename. This keeps all future milestones in actual curriculum order.

## Spoken / standard pairing

Step 15 adds 23 more codified spoken↔standard pairs through `register-pairs-step15.json`, bringing the effective policy from 81 to 104 pairs through card 1,250. Examples include:

- `از خونه کار کردن` / `از خانه کار کردن`
- `منتظر نوبت موندن` / `منتظر نوبت ماندن`
